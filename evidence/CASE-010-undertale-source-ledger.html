Evidence Ledger — CASE-010 Undertale / Toby Fox

  • Case: CASE-010
  • Status: ACTIVE
  • Last updated: 2026-10-03

E001 — Undertale Kickstarter

  • Class: P0 — contemporaneous primary
  • Source: Toby Fox, UnderTale, Kickstarter, 2013
  • URL: https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/1002143342/undertale

Campaign facts: - Goal: USD 5,000. - Pledged: USD 51,124. - Backers: 2,398. - Funding period: 2013-06-24 — 2013-07-24.

Supports: - Crowdfunding materially exceeded the stated goal and became an explicit production resource.

Does not support: - total development budget = $51,124; - crowdfunding was the first source of audience or capability.

E002 — 2013 developer-to-developer interview

  • Class: P0/P1 — near-contemporaneous direct creator interview
  • Source: Toby Fox’s Undertale – DEV 2 DEV INTERVIEW #1, 2013-05-25
  • URL: https://meloshantani.wordpress.com/2013/05/25/toby-foxs-undertale-dev-2-dev-interview-1/

Toby states: - around 2000 he and his three brothers used RPG Maker 2000 to make RPGs, though almost none were finished or released; - he made several EarthBound ROM hacks in high school; - Undertale was his first “REAL” game project; - he started composing for Homestuck in late 2009.

Supports: - C011: formal project start radically undercounts creative/technical prehistory. - C006: no AAA job did not mean zero prior capability.

E003 — Famitsu 2017 interview

  • Class: P1 — direct creator retrospective
  • Source: Famitsu, 『UNDERTALE』の開発者トビー・フォックス氏に聞く, 2017-07-12
  • URL: https://www.famitsu.com/news/201707/12137087.html

Toby states: - in December 2012, as a third-year university student, he began building a text display system and then a battle system; - while building the system, story ideas emerged; - he found Temmie Chang’s blog and directly asked her to collaborate; - the demo was completed in early 2013, followed by Kickstarter.

Supports: - student-status chronology; - Temmie is a real collaborator, not an incidental later credit; - system prototype preceded the full story plan.

E004 — Automaton 2015 interview

  • Class: P1 — direct creator interview near release
  • Source: AUTOMATON, 2D-RPG『Undertale』開発者Tobyfoxインタビュー, 2015
  • URL: https://automaton-media.com/articles/interviewsjp/undertale-developer-toby-fox-interview/

Toby states: - he had used RPG Maker / RPG Maker 2000 since around age ten for small private joke games; - before Undertale, his strongest public credential was composing for Homestuck from 2009 onward.

Supports: - long prehistory in low-cost creation and music.

E005 — Toby Fox 2025 newsletter on Homestuck debt

  • Class: P1 — creator retrospective
  • Source: Toby Fox, UNDERTALE / DELTARUNE Newsletter — Fall 2025
  • URL: https://darkgodteam.github.io/eng/newsletters/autumn25/index.html

Toby says Homestuck: - forged him into the composer he became; - gave him a social-media following that greatly helped when launching Undertale’s Kickstarter; - indirectly connected him to Temmie because he first noticed her Homestuck fanart.

Supports: - capability capital and market/social capital came from the same prehistory. - “no marketing” would erase a real audience network.

Current evidence-level conclusions

STRONGLY SUPPORTED

  • Undertale was Toby’s first major completed/released game, not his first attempt at game creation.
  • RPG Maker, ROM hacks and Homestuck preceded the formal project.
  • Kickstarter materially exceeded its goal.
  • Temmie Chang was a meaningful collaborator.
  • Homestuck contributed both compositional capability and audience/network access.

UNKNOWN

  • exact student living runway;
  • total cost / opportunity cost;
  • complete contributor map;
  • quantitative causal weight of preexisting following versus later organic spread.
