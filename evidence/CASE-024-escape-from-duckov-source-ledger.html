Evidence Ledger — CASE-024 Escape from Duckov / Team Soda

E001 — Blue Whale / Jeff interview

  • Class: P1/S1 — edited business press with direct producer interview
  • Source: 蓝鲸新闻 / Jeff（Team Soda 制作人）
  • URLs:
  • https://www.lanjinger.com/d/1760600850432686746
  • syndicated: https://www.sohu.com/a/944563082_250147

Source-derived facts: - Jeff 本科方向为数字媒体技术、研究生继续数字艺术方向；加入 Bilibili 的重要原因之一是公司给了他以制作人身份做自己游戏想法的机会； - Team Soda 在 Bilibili 内部形成，成员中包含应届生与此前并非传统游戏行业从业者的人； - 团队此前做过《蛇行武装》《碳酸危机》； - 《碳酸危机》口碑较好但商业表现不足，团队高峰约十人，成本压力促使后续更重视规模和效率； - Duckov 前期曾尝试 mobile 方向，但团队缺少移动商业化经验、自己也不是核心手游玩家，方向失败后发生调整； - 后续一度计划 PC + mobile，同时制作后发现两端玩家习惯和产品结构差异过大，于 2024 年中期砍掉 mobile； - Duckov 核心约五人，三名策划/程序向、两名美术向，多人一人多职； - Jeff 强调前作带来的工具与流程经验，称高质量工具可能带来数量级效率差异； - 团队有意识采用“一只脚在舒适区、一只脚向外探索”的方式：保留射击、卡通、易上手控制，新增 extraction、成长、大地图等风险； - Jeff 是 Tarkov 重度玩家； - 2025-10-14 左右 Steam wishlist 约 45 万； - Jeff 承认发售时 extraction 市场热度对产品有帮助，但立项时该热潮尚未成熟。

Supports: - C003: 五人规模建立在多年共同开发和前作能力之上； - C004: 团队把前作经验、工具和失败转化成下一项目的生产条件； - C007: 主动砍 mobile 并把创新集中在有限变量上； - C010: 45 万 wishlist 证明 launch 前已有可观市场基础； - C011: 两年左右的最终开发口径不能覆盖前作和失败方向的能力积累。

Boundary: - 团队规模、wishlist、前作评价主要来自制作人自述； - “工具效率十倍”是经验性表达，不应转成可审计 productivity multiplier； - 未披露内部预算、工资、利润分成。

E002 — Observer / Sina direct interview with Jeff

  • Class: P1/S1 — direct developer interview syndicated by major media
  • Source: 观察者网 / 新浪财经转载
  • Publication date: 2025-10-24
  • URL: https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2025-10-24/doc-infuyeru7133242.shtml

Source-derived facts: - 发售不到一周，销量已经超过一百万； - Jeff 说内部预期大致是 30 万“可以”、50 万“很好”，实际结果远超预期； - 团队较早判断存在想体验 Tarkov 搜索/成长但不喜欢 PvP、高惩罚的玩家； - Duckov 主动去掉大量硬核枪械/状态机制，重点保留搜索、成长、建造、任务等结构； - PVE 选择降低了被高手击败带来的挫败； - 10 月 16 日档期部分来自对 9/10/11 月大型游戏窗口的比较； - 作为 Bilibili 内部团队，五人主要承担设计/研发，其他工作由公司其他同事承担，后者对项目有实质贡献。

Supports: - C004/C007: 产品是通过有意识的机制删减和 scope 切割变成五人核心可承担结构； - C010: release timing 与 publisher/company perimeter 都是市场接入的一部分； - contributor audit: “五人开发”不能扩写成“五人完成全部 production”； - Luck: 实际销量远超制作人 base-case。

Boundary: - 销量节点为受访者/媒体同期报道，正式长期销量优先使用官方账号公告； - “30万/50万”是制作人内部预期，不是财务 break-even 数据； - 文章没有量化 Bilibili 外围投入人月。

E003 — Inven Global 2026 Jeff interview

  • Class: P1/S1 — direct developer interview
  • Source: Inven Global
  • URL: https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/23678/the-duck-syndrome-created-by-a-five-person-dev-team-escape-from-duckov

Source-derived facts: - Jeff 再次确认 Team Soda 核心为五人； - 团队成员都是 shooter 玩家并拥有不同开发经验； - Jeff 解释选择 single-player 的现实原因：对于五人团队，networked multiplayer 并不容易承担，把资源投入单机 polish 更现实； - 鸭子视觉方向是在美术风格探索中逐渐形成，随后成为项目 identity； - Steam Workshop / modding 从早期就被纳入产品设计；采访时已有 1,000+ mods。

Supports: - C007: single-player 是 scope/cost engineering，不仅是题材风格； - C004: mod/workshop 把内容扩展的一部分交给平台/社区基础设施； - Luck: 鸭子 identity 具有探索中出现而非完全事前规划的成分。

Boundary: - “1,000+ mods”是特定采访时点数据； - interview 是成功后的 retrospective，不应把所有设计选择都事后解释成唯一正确答案。

E004 — Game That Matters / TapTap direct Jeff interview

  • Class: P1/S1 — direct developer interview
  • Source: 游戏价值论 / TapTap
  • URL: https://www.taptap.cn/moment/743154344785874568

Source-derived facts: - 核心团队五人，年龄大致跨 1990–1998 出生区间； - 成员是合作 4–6 年的老同事； - 多人多职，Jeff 与另一位策划兼程序，Jeff 兼技术美术，还有成员承担音乐； - 采访把最终方向下的集中开发周期描述为约 22 个月； - 团队资源只够承担一个主要产品方向，因此没有无限并行试错。

Supports: - C003: 五人效率来自长期共同工作与技能重叠； - C011: 22 个月是最终生产窗口，不等于能力形成期； - C004: 小核心通过多职能和集中方向管理稀缺资源。

Boundary: - “22个月”需要与此前移动方向/重组时间区分； - 不应把团队成员出生年份或多职能本身解释为成功原因。

Source-derived facts: - 每日经济新闻已核首段将 TeamSoda 称为 B站旗下自研工作室； - bilibili 是商店 publisher 的平台证据另见 E006，公司外围劳动另见 E005； - 报道“内容—游戏—社区”分析是媒体解释，不是流量贡献的独立审计； - 上列待复核一手线索不作为本条新增事实。发行平台清单、自研自发表述须定位原文后再升级。

Supports: - Indie eligibility audit: Team Soda is not an external independently owned studio merely using Bilibili as an arm’s-length publisher; the public production framing is corporate internal development plus corporate publishing; - C010: owned-media/community/distribution resources belong to the project’s available market-access perimeter and must be separated from what an external indie studio could assume; - contributor / production boundary: small R&D headcount does not imply organizational or go-to-market independence.

Boundary: - current public evidence supports “Bilibili internal / self-developed / self-published” much more strongly than a generic publisher relationship, but does not expose the exact legal entity/cap-table arrangement of Team Soda as a separately incorporated subsidiary or department; - do not convert structural access into an unsupported numerical claim that a specific share of wishlists or sales came from Bilibili traffic; - BW booth allocation, individual Bilibili video views or media impressions are not by themselves causal attribution; - therefore the safe conclusion is non-independence of ownership/employment/publishing perimeter and non-comparability of market access, not an audited statement such as “X% of success was bought by Bilibili traffic.”

Current evidence-level conclusions

STRONGLY SUPPORTED

  • Duckov had a five-person core R&D team;
  • the five-person core had 4–6 years of shared work and multiple prior shipped games;
  • Team Soda formed and operated inside Bilibili; public first-party/press descriptions frame Duckov as Bilibili self-development, and bilibili is the formal publisher;
  • company colleagues outside the five-person core materially handled publishing/localization/other functions;
  • the team deliberately cut mobile and multiplayer/network complexity;
  • product design deliberately removed major Tarkov hardcore/PvP costs while retaining extraction/progression value loops;
  • launch already had roughly 450k Steam wishlists according to the producer;
  • official sales exceeded 3 million by 2025-11-08;
  • actual performance substantially exceeded the producer’s 300k/500k internal expectations;
  • Duckov should not be treated as a core example of an independently owned/self-financed/self-published studio path merely because its R&D core was small.

SUPPORTED WITH BOUNDARY

  • Soda Crisis commercial underperformance influenced later team-size/scope/tool decisions;
  • extraction-category heat at launch benefited Duckov even though the project direction predated the mature boom;
  • Workshop/modding and Bilibili’s corporate perimeter extend the effective production system beyond five people;
  • Bilibili’s content/community/distribution ecosystem was structurally available to the project, but the exact causal contribution to wishlists or sales is not publicly audited.

UNKNOWN

  • exact internal budget, burn, salaries, bonus/revenue sharing and break-even point;
  • exact legal/corporate form and IP ownership allocation for Team Soda inside the wider Bilibili organization;
  • complete contributor-hours audit across Bilibili publishing/localization/QA/marketing/platform support;
  • exact wishlist acquisition funnel by channel;
  • exact sales milestones between launch and the official 3m announcement;
  • causal contribution of Bilibili-owned media, Steam discovery, external creators, mods and overseas organic coverage to the right-tail outcome;
  • governance details explaining how Bilibili tolerated prior failure/restructuring and preserved the small-team experiment.
